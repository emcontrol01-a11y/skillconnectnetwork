Why we started

In the Volta Region, many young people have energy, passion and good ideas. Too many of them reach adulthood without employable skills, business training or access to funding.

Agriculture is Ghana's largest employer, yet it is often seen as unappealing, even though it can create wealth and jobs. We saw both gaps and set out to close them.

What began in 2020 as informal gatherings of farmers, artisans, skilled workers and young entrepreneurs in South Tongu is now a structured network. It offers hands-on training, mentorship, growth resources, funding and community partnerships in South Tongu, Central Tongu, North Tongu, Keta, Anlo, Akatsi South and Ketu North.

Vision

To be Ghana's leading network for youth empowerment and skills-based development, nurturing capable, self-reliant and socially responsible young leaders.

Mission

To connect, develop, grow and support youth and youth-owned businesses through inclusive capacity building, entrepreneurship, agribusiness development, and access to resources, funding and community-led partnerships.

How we work

Four steps take a young person from an idea to a business that lasts.

  1. Connect

    We bring together youth, farmers, artisans, mentors and partners so no one starts alone.

  2. Develop

    Hands-on training, mentorship and masterclasses build the skills the market pays for.

  3. Grow

    Incubation, market links and agribusiness support help early ventures find customers.

  4. Succeed

    Financial skills and investment readiness prepare businesses to win funding and last.

What we stand for

Seven values shape every program we run and every partnership we make.

  • Youth empowerment

    Young people are leaders, not just beneficiaries. We invest in their ideas, voices and decisions.

  • Agribusiness and entrepreneurship

    We help youth turn land, livestock and value chains into profitable businesses that create jobs.

  • Practical, market-driven skills

    Vocational, digital, technical and business training that matches what the market needs.

  • Inclusivity and equal access

    We reach young women, rural youth, school dropouts and persons with disabilities.

  • Community-led partnership

    We work with local leaders, government, the private sector and development partners.

  • Social responsibility and civic leadership

    We inspire young people to solve local problems and look out for their communities.

  • Integrity and stewardship

    Strict financial accountability, transparency and care for the environment.

Our programs

Our work is organised around three pillars.

Youth and skills development

We build people first, with practical skills, leadership and market-ready know-how.

Youth development
Inclusive programs that help young leaders become active, self-reliant contributors to their communities.
Skills development
Vocational, technical and practical training that follows market demand and industry standards.
Women empowerment
Targeted work on gender equity, women-led enterprises and economic independence.
Capacity building and training
Workshops, mentoring sessions and masterclasses for individuals and organisations.

Enterprise and sector growth

We speed up business creation and open up opportunities across key sectors.

Agribusiness development
Support for agricultural entrepreneurs and smallholders through value-chain growth, modern practices and market links.
Business incubation
Mentorship, infrastructure and strategic guidance for early-stage enterprises and startups.
Market development
Trade links, access to new consumers and stronger local distribution channels.

Financial and operational readiness

We help ventures stay sustainable, fundable and well run.

Financial literacy and management
Budgeting, financial tracking and cash-flow skills for entrepreneurs and youth.
Investment readiness
Preparing growing businesses to secure funding, work with investors and meet compliance standards.
Data management assistance
Helping ventures capture, organise and analyse data for better decisions and impact reporting.

Recent trainings

A look at some of the hands-on sessions we have hosted.

Gari production and food safety

Training on gari production, good manufacturing practices, food safety and traceability improvements. Participants went from peeling and grating cassava to roasting on clay stoves, and practised hygiene in hairnets and face masks.

  • Agribusiness
  • Food safety
  • Traceability
  • Good manufacturing practices

Business development and financial management

Training on business development and financial management, with case studies and sessions on business ethics for real business decisions.

  • Business skills
  • Finance
  • Business ethics
  • Case studies

Pottery and souvenir making

Training on pot production, glazing, market expansion and the moulding of souvenirs. Participants worked clay by hand and on the wheel, practised glazing, and studied finished pieces for the market.

  • Crafts
  • Glazing
  • Market expansion
  • Souvenirs

Contract farming and agribusiness

Training on contract farming and agribusiness development, mapping who does what along the value chain. Participants worked in groups on workshop boards covering planning tools, contract farming models, management structure and risk.

  • Farming
  • Value chains
  • Contract farming
  • Group workshops

Common questions

Not sure where to start? These answers cover the basics. For anything else, call or message us.

Who can join SCN programs?

Our programs are open to youth and women, with a focus on last-mile actors: the unemployed and underemployed, single parents, school dropouts, persons with disabilities, widows, orphans and less-privileged people.

Where do you work?

We are headquartered in Sogakope and reach youth in South Tongu, Central Tongu, North Tongu, Keta, Anlo, Akatsi South and Ketu North.

What kinds of training do you offer?

Recent sessions covered gari production and food safety, business development and financial management, pottery and souvenir making, and contract farming. We also run financial literacy, investment readiness and data management support.

How can my organisation partner with SCN?

We build community-led partnerships with local leaders, government, the private sector and development partners. Email skillsconnectnetwork@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp and we will respond.

How is my donation used?

Donations support our training, mentorship and enterprise programs. We keep strict standards of financial accountability and transparency across every project.
